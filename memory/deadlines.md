# Deadlines

One row per deadline, sorted by date, written in the user's language. Kept by the skill
`deadlines`; the startup script warns when a row with status `open` falls within 7 days.
Keep the format: `| YYYY-MM-DD | what | job or source | status |` (status: open / done).

| Date | What | Job or source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
