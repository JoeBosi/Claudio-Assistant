# Privacy — data of clients, suppliers and the office

The professional is the **controller** of the personal data that reaches the office
(clients, suppliers, collaborators, other people mentioned in documents). Keeping and using
it for their assignments is lawful: it is needed to perform the contract and to meet legal
obligations (data protection law, e.g. the GDPR in the EU).

## Working rules (always)
1. **Kept, not disclosed.** The data stays on this computer and on the office server or
   cloud. Never on external websites, sharing services, emails or messages unless the user
   asks.
2. **Web searches and external tools**: only laws, procedures, products, public addresses.
   Never names, tax codes, VAT numbers, phones, emails or amounts of clients or suppliers.
3. **Bare minimum in memory**: in `contacts/` only the data needed to work (company name,
   contact person, work contact details, how they behave).
4. **Sensitive data** (health, ID documents, bank details, judicial data): used only inside
   the document that requires it, never in memory, never in the examples without need. If
   it arrives for no reason, flag it to the user.
5. **Closed jobs**: after archiving on the server, the local folder goes to
   `jobs/_archived/` (the card stays, so everything can be found).
6. **Rights of the people concerned**: if someone asks what data we hold or asks for it to
   be deleted, use the skill `forget` (full search, then the user decides what to remove:
   some documents must be kept by law).
7. Passwords, PINs, digital signature codes: never, anywhere.
