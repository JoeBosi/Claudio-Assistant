LEARN-FROM-HERE - the inbox for Claude

Put here the work that turned out well and that you want Claude to learn to redo.

You can put in:
  - just the finished document (PDF, Word, Excel, photos, drawings);
  - the document plus a note of yours (a text file, even two lines:
    "this is the report for client X, look at how I wrote the conclusions");
  - a whole folder with all the files of one job.

Notes can be written in your own language.

Then open Claude and write: "I've put some work in the inbox"
(or /learn-from-here). If you forget, Claude notices by itself
at the next chat and suggests it.

Claude studies the files, asks you to confirm, moves them to the
"good examples" and the inbox is empty again. It never deletes anything.
