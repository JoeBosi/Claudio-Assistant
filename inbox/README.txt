Drop here the emails you want Claudio to read: drag them from your mail program
(Outlook, Thunderbird, Apple Mail, Gmail "Download message") into this folder, or
save them as .eml, .msg, .txt or .pdf. Then tell Claudio "read the mail".
Processed emails are moved to the job folder (jobs/<job>/received/) and a copy
stays in inbox/_done/. Nothing is deleted and nothing is sent.
